## Membership Guidelines

### Who Should Be in Multiple Groups?

**Example: Owner-occupant on Boulder Circle**
- ✅ bouldercircle@ (their street)
- ✅ owneroccupant@ (their status)
- ✅ owners@ (master owner list)
- ✅ volunteers@ (if active volunteer)

**Example: Non-occupant owner**
- ✅ [their street]@ (geographic)
- ✅ nonoccupantowner@ (their status)
- ✅ owners@ (master owner list)
- ❌ NOT in owneroccupant@

**Example: Renter**
- ✅ [their street]@ (for community info)
- ✅ nonowneroccupant@ (their status)
- ❌ NOT in owners@
- ❌ NOT in owneroccupant@
- ✅ volunteers@ (if they volunteer)

## Adding/Removing Members

### Via Contacts Labels

**To Add Someone:**
1. Add contact to Google Contacts
2. Apply appropriate label (e.g., "Boulder Circle")
3. Run sync (or wait for daily sync)
4. Verify member appears in group

**To Remove Someone:**
1. Remove label from contact in Google Contacts
2. Run sync
3. Verify member removed from group

**To Change Groups:**
1. Update labels in Contacts
2. Run sync
3. All groups update automatically

## Email Usage Examples

**To:** lbc@villasboulders.org

### Winter Emergency
**To:** snowsquad@villasboulders.org
