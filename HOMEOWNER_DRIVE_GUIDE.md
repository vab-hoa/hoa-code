# VaB HOA — Homeowner Documents Guide

The HOA maintains a shared Google Drive folder — **VaB Homeowner Documents** — where you can find governing documents, budgets, financials, meeting minutes, newsletters, policies, maps, and forms. This is the official electronic records library for the community.

---

## What You Need

Access requires a Google account. Most people use a Gmail address, but a Google account can be tied to any email address.

**You must use the Google account you registered with the HOA.** Access is granted to that specific account. If you're not sure which one it is, contact the president at president@villasboulders.org.

---

## Getting There on a Computer

1. Open a browser and go to **[drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)**.
2. Make sure you're signed into the right Google account. Your account icon or initial appears in the top-right corner — click it to check or switch accounts.
3. In the left sidebar, look for **Shared drives**. Click it.
4. You should see **VaB Homeowner Documents** listed. Click it to open.

> **Don't see Shared drives in the sidebar?** It may be collapsed. Look for a small arrow or "More" link below the main menu items and click to expand.

> **Don't see VaB Homeowner Documents in the list?** Your account hasn't been granted access yet, or you're signed into the wrong Google account. Contact the president at president@villasboulders.org.

---

## Getting There on a Phone or Tablet

1. Install the **Google Drive** app if you don't have it (available free on iOS and Android).
2. Open the app and make sure you're signed into the right Google account (tap the account icon in the top-right to check).
3. Tap the menu icon (≡) in the top-left corner.
4. Tap **Shared drives**.
5. Tap **VaB Homeowner Documents**.

---

## What's Inside

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Budgets** | Annual budget presentations |
| **Financials** | Monthly financial reports from Aurora Property Management |
| **Governing Documents** | Bylaws, Covenants (CC&Rs), ARC Design Guidelines, Articles of Incorporation |
| **Meetings** | Published meeting minutes (PDF) |
| **Newsletters** | Monthly newsletters |
| **Policies** | Officially adopted policies (PDF) |
| **Reserve Studies** | Reserve fund studies (2006, 2015, 2022) |
| **Maps** | Street maps, plat map, elevation drawings |
| **Forms** | ARC application, proxy form, LBC application |
| **Projects** | Information on major community projects |

---

## Viewing and Downloading Files

Click any file to open it in your browser. For PDFs, Google Drive has a built-in viewer — no special software needed.

To download a file to your computer: open it, then click the download icon (↓) or use **File → Download**.

You cannot edit or delete files in this drive — it is read-only for homeowners.
